Definition: Reductive Group
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-2291a7d34fe29f04"}
A connected smooth affine algebraic group $G$ over an algebraically closed field is reductive if its unipotent radical $R_u(G)$ is trivial. Equivalently, $G$ has no nontrivial connected normal unipotent subgroup.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-53dca0d6ed3f787e" tags="definition"}
Definition: Algebraic Group
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-53dca0d6ed3f787e"}
An algebraic group over a field $k$ is a group object in the category of $k$-varieties: multiplication $G\times_kG\to G$, inverse $G\to G$, and identity $\operatorname{Spec}k\to G$ are morphisms satisfying the group axioms.
:::
