::: {.front card-id="card-bcba6e654f0b5155" tags="definition"}
Definition: Picard Group
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-bcba6e654f0b5155"}
The group of invertible sheaves (or line bundles) on $X$ up to isomorphism equipped with $\tensor$
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-b54646603b961630" tags="definition"}
Definition: Ideal Class Group of an algebraic number field $K$
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-b54646603b961630"}
The group of fractional ideals of the ring of integers of $K$ mod the subgroup of principal ideals. 
Trivial iff all ideals are principal.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-0c9125c6959114e2" tags="definition"}
Definition: Global Field
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-0c9125c6959114e2"}
A global field is either a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ or a finite extension of $\mathbb{F}_q(t)$ for some finite field $\mathbb{F}_q$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-e01d6cc90a3752e3" tags="definition"}
Definition: Specializations
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-e01d6cc90a3752e3"}
For points $x,y$ of a scheme $X$, $y$ is a specialization of $x$ if $y\in\overline{\{x\}}$. Equivalently, $x$ is a generalization of $y$. In $\operatorname{Spec}R$, $\mathfrak p$ specializes to $\mathfrak q$ exactly when $\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak q$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-191c32e34e7db670" tags="definition"}
Definition: Height of an Ideal
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-191c32e34e7db670"}
The height of a prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ is the supremum of lengths of strict chains $\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak p_n=\mathfrak p$. For an ideal $I$, $\operatorname{ht}(I)=\inf\{\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p):\mathfrak p\supseteq I\text{ prime}\}$.
:::
