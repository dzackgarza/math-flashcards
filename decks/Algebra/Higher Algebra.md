How is ${\operatorname{HH}}^*_{{\mathbb{E}}_n}(A)$ defined for $A\in { {}_{{\mathbb{E}}_n} \mathsf{Alg}}$?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-bcc1cd767d2b3471"}
Essentially $\int_{S^n} A$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-ca452e66e8eec59e"}
Describe factorization homology.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-ca452e66e8eec59e"}
A homology theory for framed $n$-manifolds with coefficients given by $\mathcal{E}_n$-algebras, constructed as a topological analogue of Beilinson-Drinfeld's chiral homology (for factorization coalgebras, motivated by conformal field theory).
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-8440d313e9334d60"}
Describe an ${\mathbb{E}}_n\dash$algebra.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-8440d313e9334d60"}
Algebras with multiplication maps parametrized by configuration spaces of $n$-dimensional disks inside a standard $n$-disk.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-dd88ae25fc67ab73"}
Describe a braided monoidal category in terms of ${\mathbb{E}}_n\dash$algebras.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-dd88ae25fc67ab73"}
An ${\mathbb{E}}_2\dash$algebra object in $\mathsf{Cat}$, since ${\mathbb{E}}_2(n) = {\mathbf{B}}B_n$ where $B_n$ is the pure braid group.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-0d21d41c6c446761"}
What are the higher classifying spaces ${\mathbf{B}}^n A$ for $A\in { {}_{{\mathbb{E}}_n} \mathsf{Alg}}$?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-0d21d41c6c446761"}
A $\mathsf{C}\dash$enriched $(\infty, n)\dash$category whose object is a point, a single $k\dash$morphism $\phi^k$ for each $1\leq k\leq n-1$, and ${\mathbf{B}}^nA(\phi^{n-1}, \phi^{n-1})\simeq A$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-d18ecf0f12783367"}
Describe $\Omega_A$ algebraically.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-d18ecf0f12783367"}
$I/I^2$ where $I \coloneqq\ker(A{ {}^{ \scriptscriptstyle\otimes_{k}^{2} }  }\to A)$.

Corepresents derivations: $\mathop{\mathrm{Der}}_k(A, {-}) \cong \mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}_A(\Omega_A, {-})$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-a96c7a0d3d74adbd"}
Describe ${\mathbb{L}}$ algebraically.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-a96c7a0d3d74adbd"}
For $A$ nonsmooth, $\mathop{\mathrm{Der}}_k(A, {-})$ is not right exact, and has Quillen right-derived functors corepresented by ${\mathbb{L}}_A$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-d3f919f3fb3b66f9"}
What is a presentable $\infty\dash$category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-d3f919f3fb3b66f9"}
Closed under small colimits and limits, and weakly generated by a small subcategory.

Given by localizations of $\infty\dash$categories of presheaves on a small $\infty\dash$category.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-0c23faaaf479dbdb"}
What is a continuous functor?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-0c23faaaf479dbdb"}
Preserves all colimits.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-873bcf13f5dd4208"}
Give a precise definition of factorization homology.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-873bcf13f5dd4208"}
For a framed $n$-manifold $M$ and a $\operatorname{Disk}_n^{\mathrm{fr}}$-algebra $A$ in a symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category admitting the relevant colimits, factorization homology is $\int_M A=\colim_{(U\hookrightarrow M)\in\operatorname{Disk}_n^{\mathrm{fr}}/M}A(U)$. Here $U$ ranges over finite disjoint unions of framed $n$-disks, and $A(U)$ is the tensor product of one copy of $A$ for each component.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-5c678f8b5d731b50"}
What motivates construction of the Tate diagonal?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-5c678f8b5d731b50"}
${\mathsf{Sp}}$ does not have a diagonal map, $X\to X{ {}^{ \scriptstyle\otimes_{}^{2} }  }$ which factors through homotopy fixed points $(X{ {}^{ \scriptstyle\otimes_{}^{2} }  })^{hC_2}\to X{ {}^{ \scriptstyle\otimes_{}^{2} }  }$ (i.e. is symmetric), although this does exist for $\Sigma_+^\infty X$ for $X\in \Top$ induced by the diagonal of spaces.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-99e54636d46d62ff"}
Define the Tate diagonal.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-99e54636d46d62ff"}
The natural transformation
$$ \Delta_p: \mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{Sp}} \rightarrow T_p: X \rightarrow(X \otimes \ldots \otimes X)^{t C_p}$$
of endofunctors of $\mathrm{Sp}$ which corresponds to the map
$$\mathbb{S} \rightarrow T_p(\mathbb{S})=\mathbb{S}^{t C_p}$$
which is the composition $\mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}^{h C_p} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}^{t C_p}$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-7f04f673834a95af"}
What is the right analog of ${ \ZZ_{\widehat{p}} }$ in ${\mathsf{Sp}}$?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-7f04f673834a95af"}
${\mathbf{S}}^{tC_p}$, since $H\ZZ^{tC_p}$ is not connective.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-d66a92114e3de17e"}
Motivate the passage from schemes to derived stacks.
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-d66a92114e3de17e"}
$\Sch\to{\mathsf{St}}$: closure under taking quotients and other colimits

${\mathsf{St}}\to \mathsf{dSt}$: closure under taking fiber products and other limits

$\Sch\to \mathsf{dSt}:$ closure under "imposing equations".

Think of $X\in {\mathsf{dSt}}$ as a functor $F_X: \mathsf{dCRing}\to \Top$.

Think of source as connective cdgas, more generally connective ${\mathbb{E}}_\infty\dash$ring spectra.
:::

Perfect, dualizable, or compact objects.

What is the derived version of the center of an algebra $Z(A)$?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-bd021a74625d7f56"}
${\operatorname{HH}}^*(A)$, which computes $\mathop{\mathrm{\mathbb{R}Hom}}_{({A}, {A})\dash\mathsf{biMod}}(A, A)$ (the derived $(A,A)\dash$bimodule endomorphism algebra).

::: {.front card-id="card-87669b93022e2e74"}
What is the free loop space of a derived stack?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-87669b93022e2e74"}
$\mcl X \coloneqq\mathop{\mathrm{Maps}}(X, S^1) = X \underset{\scriptscriptstyle {X^e} }{\times} X$ where $X^e \coloneqq X \underset{\scriptscriptstyle {k} }{\times}X$.

What is $\mcl X$ when $X\in \Sch_{/ {k}}$ in characteristic zero?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-6f1f924a5e5be913"}
$\mcl X = {\mathbf{T}}_X[-1] = \operatorname{Spec}\operatorname{Sym}^*\Omega_X[1]$.

What is $\mcl{{\mathbf{B}}G}$?

::: {.back card-id="card-84d9affed946f205"}
The adjoint quotient $G/G$.

What is the stacky version of a $G\dash$local system on $X$?

$\mathsf{Loc}_G(X) \cong \mathop{\mathrm{Maps}}(X, {{\mathbf{B}}G})$.

${\mathsf{ho}}\mathsf{C}$ has a canonical triangulated structure for $\mathsf{C}$ a stable infty cat.

What does it mean for a functor $F:\mathsf{C}\times \mathsf{D}\to \mathsf{E}$ to be bilinear?

::: {.back card-id="card-413064f0e0c62ddc"}
Factors through $\mathsf{C}\otimes\mathsf{D}$, commutes with colimits in both variables separately.

::: {.front card-id="card-e2eae38bad91f656"}
What is the sheaf condition for a derived stack?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-e2eae38bad91f656"}
$X\in \Fun( {}_{k}  \mathsf{Alg}, \Top)$ where if $A\to B$ is an etale cover, $X(A)  { \, \xrightarrow{\sim}\, }\lim X(\tilde B)$ is an equivalence where $\tilde B\rightrightarrows B$ is the cosimplicial bar resolution induced by ${-}\otimes_A B$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-eb8f2156f1b4dcd3"}
What is a compact object?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-eb8f2156f1b4dcd3"}
An object $M$ of a stable $\infty$-category $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be compact if $\operatorname{Hom}_\mcc(M,-)$ commutes with all coproducts (equivalently, with all colimits).

$M$ is compact iff any map $M\to A$ where $A$ is a small coproduct factors through a finite coproduct, when $\mathsf{C}$ is stable presentable.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-15e2a11f614e5e0e"}
What is a dualizable object?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-15e2a11f614e5e0e"}
(2) An object $M$ of a stable symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be (strongly) dualizable if there is an object $M^{\vee}$ and unit and trace maps
$$1 \stackrel{u}{\longrightarrow} M \otimes M^{\vee} \stackrel{\tau}{\longrightarrow} 1$$
such that the composite map
$$M \stackrel{u \otimes \mathrm{id}}{\longrightarrow} M \otimes M^{\vee} \otimes M \stackrel{\mathrm{id} \otimes \tau}{\longrightarrow} M$$
is the identity.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-0bcb098e3ee04582"}
What is the usefulness of dualizable objects?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-0bcb098e3ee04582"}
If $M$ has a dual $M^\vee$, maps out of $M$ can be transposed: $\operatorname{Hom}(M\otimes X,Y)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(X,M^\vee\otimes Y)$. Thus tensoring with $M$ has a tensor adjoint, and evaluation and coevaluation let one define traces of endomorphisms of $M$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-a791584e0ac1271e" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of (infinity,n)-category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-a791584e0ac1271e"}
A higher category in which all $k$-morphisms are invertible for $k>n$, so non-invertible morphisms occur only up to level $n$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-4f9dea568ce92121" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Adjoint quotient?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-4f9dea568ce92121"}
For a group $G$ acting on itself by conjugation, the quotient stack $[G/G]$, equivalently the free loop space $\mathcal L(BG)$; it classifies $G$-bundles on the circle, equivalently $G$-torsors equipped with an automorphism.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-2e34b888a08cdc5f" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Cdga?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-2e34b888a08cdc5f"}
A commutative differential graded algebra: a graded commutative algebra $A$ with a degree-$1$ differential $d$ satisfying $d(ab)=d(a)b+(-1)^{|a|}a\,d(b)$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-3b19794fd92da0a9" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Chiral homology?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-3b19794fd92da0a9"}
The factorization homology of a factorization coalgebra; a topological/chiral analogue of Beilinson-Drinfeld chiral homology.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-f1ad77a2b8c5fcc0" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Classifying space?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-f1ad77a2b8c5fcc0"}
For a topological group $G$, a space $BG$ with a principal $G$-bundle $EG\to BG$ that is universal; for discrete $G$, $BG=K(G,1)$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-7dcd9b8fd146d7f3" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Coevaluation?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-7dcd9b8fd146d7f3"}
For a dualizable object $M$ with dual $M^\vee$, the unit map $1\to M\otimes M^\vee$ exhibiting $M^\vee$ as a dual of $M$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-16128e388f458f16" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Configuration space?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-16128e388f458f16"}
The space $\operatorname{Conf}_k(M)=\{(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\in M^k:x_i\neq x_j\text{ for }i\neq j\}$ of $k$ distinct ordered points in $M$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-c266b7a4025a6e2f" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Connective?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-c266b7a4025a6e2f"}
A spectrum $X$ with $\pi_n(X)=0$ for $n<0$; a connective ring spectrum is one whose underlying spectrum is connective.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-38f050718dfbb5a7" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Cosimplicial object?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-38f050718dfbb5a7"}
A functor $\Delta\to\mathcal C$, where $\Delta$ is the simplex category; the cosimplicial bar resolution is built from such an object.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-8194f88a835b7153" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Derived center?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-8194f88a835b7153"}
The derived endomorphism algebra $\operatorname{RHom}_{(A,A)\text{-bimod}}(A,A)$, equivalent to Hochschild cohomology $\operatorname{HH}^*(A)$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-87df13960aa92cf4" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Derived stack?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-87df13960aa92cf4"}
A functor from connective $E_\infty$-rings (or connective cdgas) to spaces satisfying etale descent; a derived enhancement of a stack.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-a9986e2c5f6331d2" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of E-infinity ring spectrum?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-a9986e2c5f6331d2"}
A commutative ring spectrum, i.e. an algebra over the $E_\infty$ operad in spectra; a highly structured refinement of a commutative ring in the stable homotopy category.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-46f42be3a2518ba8" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Enriched category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-46f42be3a2518ba8"}
A category $\mathcal C$ whose Hom-objects lie in a monoidal category $\mathcal V$, with composition morphisms $\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(Y,Z)\to\operatorname{Hom}(X,Z)$ in $\mathcal V$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-06e35aac7475b65e" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Evaluation map?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-06e35aac7475b65e"}
For a dualizable object $M$ with dual $M^\vee$, the map $\operatorname{ev}:M\otimes M^\vee\to 1$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-2f13d8a819f5d05e" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Factorization coalgebra?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-2f13d8a819f5d05e"}
A coalgebra object in a category of $D$-modules (or sheaves) on the Ran space equipped with factorization isomorphisms, encoding chiral operations.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-73aaa2edf7f6bddb" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Framed n-manifold?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-73aaa2edf7f6bddb"}
A smooth $n$-manifold $M$ together with a trivialization of its tangent bundle, i.e. a global framing.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-94b31056a61ecf43" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Homotopy category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-94b31056a61ecf43"}
The category obtained from a category with weak equivalences by formally inverting those weak equivalences (e.g. $\operatorname{Ho}(\mathcal C)$ of a model category).
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-469f5cf8c7443c58" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Homotopy fixed points?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-469f5cf8c7443c58"}
For a group $G$ acting on a space or spectrum $X$, the derived fixed points $X^{hG}=\operatorname{Map}^G(EG,X)$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-4793e4f11906cccf" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Kahler differentials?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-4793e4f11906cccf"}
The module $\Omega_{A/k}$ generated by symbols $da$ for $a\in A$, modulo $d(ab)=a\,db+b\,da$ and $d(ka)=k\,da$; it corepresents derivations, $\operatorname{Der}_k(A,M)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_A(\Omega_{A/k},M)$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-e3ba668d9b4ff212" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Little n-disks operad?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-e3ba668d9b4ff212"}
The operad $\mathcal D_n$ whose $k$-ary operations are configurations of $k$ disjoint standard $n$-disks inside the unit $n$-disk, composed by insertion.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-04a30c2fe605e3f3" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Monoidal category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-04a30c2fe605e3f3"}
A category $\mathcal C$ with a bifunctor $\otimes:\mathcal C\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$, a unit object $1$, and natural associativity and unit isomorphisms satisfying coherence.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-c64c0d8ff960be67" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Operad?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-c64c0d8ff960be67"}
A sequence of objects $\mathcal O(n)$ with $\Sigma_n$-actions and composition maps $\mathcal O(k)\times\mathcal O(n_1)\times\cdots\times\mathcal O(n_k)\to\mathcal O(n_1+\cdots+n_k)$ encoding $n$-ary operations and their substitution; an $\mathcal O$-algebra has operations $\mathcal O(n)\otimes A^{\otimes n}\to A$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-78887401d4cafec1" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Perfect object?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-78887401d4cafec1"}
An object of a stable $\infty$-category (e.g. $D(X)$) that is dualizable; equivalently, locally a bounded complex of finitely generated projective modules.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-db0391ea15ee1c2b" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Pure braid group?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-db0391ea15ee1c2b"}
The fundamental group of the configuration space of $n$ ordered points in the plane; it is the kernel of the surjection $B_n\to\Sigma_n$ from the braid group to the symmetric group.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-38388f12ef667589" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Smooth algebra?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-38388f12ef667589"}
A $k$-algebra $A$ that is formally smooth: every nilpotent extension $B\to B/I$ and map $A\to B/I$ lifts to $B$. Equivalently, $\Omega_{A/k}$ is projective and the cotangent complex is concentrated in degree $0$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-b1a6b8c044cade92" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Stack?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-b1a6b8c044cade92"}
A sheaf of groupoids on a site (e.g. the etale site of schemes), i.e. a presheaf satisfying descent up to coherent isomorphism.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-5f31d9e18483f286" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Suspension spectrum?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-5f31d9e18483f286"}
The spectrum $\Sigma^\infty X$ with $(\Sigma^\infty X)_n=\Sigma^nX$ and identity structure maps, associated to a based space $X$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-8e2af1243a5ff75e" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Symmetric monoidal category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-8e2af1243a5ff75e"}
A monoidal category $(\mathcal C,\otimes,1)$ equipped with a natural isomorphism $X\otimes Y\cong Y\otimes X$ satisfying the hexagon and coherence axioms.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-af57818899ee9a1d" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Tate construction?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-af57818899ee9a1d"}
For a spectrum $X$ with $G$-action, the Tate spectrum $X^{tG}=\operatorname{cofib}(X_{hG}\to X^{hG})$; the Tate diagonal maps $X\to(X^{\otimes p})^{tC_p}$.
:::

::: {.front card-id="card-0ac4036f79339052" tags="definition"}
What is the definition of Triangulated category?
:::

::: {.back card-id="card-0ac4036f79339052"}
An additive category equipped with a shift functor $[1]$ and a class of distinguished triangles $X\to Y\to Z\to X[1]$ satisfying the axioms (TR1)-(TR4): closure under isomorphisms, the cone construction, and the octahedral axiom.
:::
